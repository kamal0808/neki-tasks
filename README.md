# Neki tasks

Neki's public project repo. Neki is how every TOPC project (Ideato, Founderbot and others) records who did what: 1 Neki = 1 rupee of work at market price, and your share of a project = your Neki on it / all Neki on it. This project is Neki itself: the coin, its name and look, the rate card, scoring and the ledger rules.

- [Issues](https://github.com/kamal0808/neki-tasks/issues): open tasks. Each issue is one task, and its `neki:N` label is the Neki it earns.
- [`LEDGER.md`](LEDGER.md): who has earned what on Neki.

What Neki means for the person earning it: https://www.ideato.social/neki

## How it works

1. Pick any open issue. Anyone can hand in, and the best hand-in wins.
2. Do the work however you like, including with your own AI.
3. Hand in by commenting on the issue with a link others can open and a line or two on what you did.
4. Kamal accepts the best hand-in, labels the issue `accepted`, and closes it. The Neki is added as a row in [`LEDGER.md`](LEDGER.md).

## Rules

- Proof must be something others can open. Work done before tasks existed needs one witness besides the person claiming it, confirming on the task, or it stays provisional.
- Made-up people, answers or numbers earn nothing.
