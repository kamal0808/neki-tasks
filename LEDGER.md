# Neki ledger

Neki's own project ledger: Neki earned for work on Neki itself (the coin, its name and look, the rate card, scoring, the ledger rules). A person's share of Neki = their Neki here / all Neki here. Rules: TOPC's NEKI.md.

Units: 1 Neki = 1 rupee of work at market price. Rows are only ever added, never edited; corrections are new rows. Status is `provisional` until an outside judge reviews it, then `final`.

The founding work on Neki moves here from TOPC's ledger first, so later rows are a real slice and not the whole.
